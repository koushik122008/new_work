## 2026-07-22 - Optimizing Box3 allocations in nested loops
**Learning:** Instantiating `THREE.Box3` inside the rendering loop, particularly within nested loops (like projectile vs enemy collision), creates a tremendous amount of garbage collection pressure. Additionally, calling `setFromObject` recursively traverses all children of a 3D object to compute its bounding box, compounding the O(Projectiles * Enemies) performance cost.
**Action:** Always pre-allocate and reuse temporary variables like `THREE.Box3` globally or via closure scope when performing per-frame bounding box checks. In scenarios with a many-to-many relationship (like projectiles hitting enemies), calculate and cache the bounding boxes in the outer/linear loop (O(N) operation) and simply check for intersection in the nested O(N*M) loop.
